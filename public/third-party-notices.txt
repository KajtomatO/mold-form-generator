==============================================================================
3. Compiled into manifold-3d 3.5.4 (manifold.wasm and manifold.js)
==============================================================================

manifold-3d's authors build manifold.wasm with Emscripten. Besides Manifold
itself (above), the WebAssembly holds the components below. Manifold
3.5.4's own sources name them: Clipper2, which its build fetches, and code
in its own headers taken from linalg.h, PhysX, tbtSVD, quickhull, dset and
fdlibm. Emscripten adds its runtime and embind, its C library, which is
based on musl, and LLVM's libc++ and libc++abi. The npm package does not
record their versions.
