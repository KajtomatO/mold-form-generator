# The example gallery's models

The models the app offers under "Or try an example" and in the Model section's Examples panel. This file is their one notice: the software the app is built with is listed in `third-party-notices.txt`, not here.

The cube, sphere, cylinder and cone are made by Mold Form Generator's own `scripts/make-gallery-primitives.mjs`, each 40 mm tall, and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: use them for anything, with no credit needed. Their thumbnails in `thumbs/` are drawn from them by `scripts/make-help-media.mjs` and are CC0 1.0 too.

A model the project does not own keeps its author's licence, which allows any use, commercial use and changes included. The app shows its credit, the author, the model's page and the licence, while it is on screen, on its card, in the README.txt of an export and in a saved project.
